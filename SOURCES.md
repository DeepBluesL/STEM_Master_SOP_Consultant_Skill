# Source notes

Editorial references include Erica Weng's Tips and Tricks, How-To Guide for Grad School SoPs (a user-supplied copy), [MIT EECS Communication Lab](https://mitcommlab.mit.edu/eecs/commkit/graduate-school-statement-of-purpose/), [Mehtaverse](https://mehtaver.se/how-to-write-a-statement-of-purpose-for-grad-school/), and [WriteIvy](https://writeivy.com/structure-is-magic-a-guide-to-the-graduate-sop/).

Architecture and selected workflow ideas were adapted from [Haadhi Irfan's SOP_Consultant](https://github.com/Haadhi76/SOP_Consultant), under its preserved MIT license. Instructions were independently written for STEM master's applications.

The local qualitative corpus contains 20 application versions by Sai Siddartha Maram, Anant Bhardwaj's MS statement, and Vaishnavi Khindkar's MSCV statement. Admission evidence varies by document. The analysis treats repeated versions as one author family and examines rhetorical choices. Corpus identifiers in the narrative reference refer to that local analysis.
