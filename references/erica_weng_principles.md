## Adapting the research-specific advice

Use the official limit to set length. Weng's two-page suggestion and page allocations describe her research-oriented approach; the transferable principle is selective use of space.

For a thesis MS with a documented supervisor-selection process, apply her advice on reading faculty and student work and connecting it to prior preparation. For a professional program, investigate courses, capstone structure, and applied development first. Treat advisor working style as a topic for conversations and published lab guidance.

Give technical internships substantial space when they demonstrate the competencies the master's program develops. Weng's research-first ordering serves her PhD setting; professional master's essays benefit from engineering ownership and deployment experience.

Her examples illustrate three especially useful transformations: compressing the journey toward an insight into the insight itself; replacing a cluster of broad qualities with a concrete function; and replacing an abstract desire with a direct first-person intention. These transformations preserve the underlying fact while making its rhetorical job clearer.
