## Useful variations

An applicant with one substantial project can build depth through successive questions and decisions. An applicant with several projects can show a changing method or a recurring constraint. A professional applicant can organize around increasingly complex engineering responsibility. A cross-field applicant can make the transfer explicit and identify the new domain knowledge they seek.

## Lessons from weaker passages

Several Sai Maram versions repeat broad praise about research, empathy, and global impact. Concrete experience-to-resource connections provide stronger adaptation. PDF21's USC discussion includes both the Interaction Lab and H2R terminology; use that inconsistency as a cue to verify every carried-over affiliation. PDF25's quantum-inspired paragraph introduces a new conceptual direction late in the essay; develop its evidence and relevance if the applicant chooses to keep it.

The corpus supports qualitative editorial observations. Source notes describe the evidence base.
